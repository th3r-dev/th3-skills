# Marketing frameworks

The few frameworks the strategy sub-skill uses, with where each came from. Practitioner blogs are
labelled as such; treat their numbers as rules of thumb.

## Strategy stack

From Kasidis Satangmongkol, ["Digital Marketing 101"](https://datarockie.com/blog/turn-pro-digital-marketing/) (DataRockie, 2024), top down:

1. Business objective and strategy
2. Communication objective and strategy
3. Creative ideas and content
4. Communication plan and media channels
5. Execution and evaluation

Strategy is getting from where you are to where you want to be with the least effort, building an
advantage others can't copy easily. Selling and branding can run together ("brandformance").
A product that sells itself (product-market fit) comes before spending on media.

## STP, value proposition, persona

From Salesforce, ["Marketing strategy"](https://www.salesforce.com/ap/marketing/strategy/):

- **STP:** segment the market (demographics, psychographics, behaviour, geography), target one
  segment, position the offer in it.
- **Value proposition:** why a buyer should pick this service.
- **Buyer persona:** a profile of the ideal client that steers positioning and content.
- **SMART goals:** specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, timely.
- **Eight steps:** research, goals, audience, competitors, key messages, channels, KPIs, the plan.
- Strategy is the plan; tactics are the actions (a post, a listing image).

## Marketing mix

4Ps (Jerome McCarthy, 1960): product, price, place, promotion. Services add people, process and
physical evidence (7Ps), per DataRockie. For a freelance listing: the package is the product, the
platform is the place, the process image and work samples are the physical evidence.

## Funnel and metrics

From DataRockie:

| Stage | Buyer | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| ToFu, awareness | sees the image or post | impressions, reach |
| MoFu, consideration | reads, opens samples | clicks, engagement |
| BoFu, conversion | chats, orders | leads, orders |

AIDA (awareness, interest, desire, action) is the same path. Keeping an existing customer costs
4 to 5 times less than winning a new one (cited in DataRockie), so the funnel ends in retention:
support, then an ongoing package. Ad formulas when paid ads come in: CTR = clicks / impressions,
CPA = spend / leads, ROAS = revenue / spend.

## First clients on a marketplace

Practitioner advice (Upwork and gig-platform guides, 2026; not research):

- Narrow the first profile to the strongest skill; widen after reviews.
- Reviews are the main gap for a new seller: a small, clearly scoped starter offer wins them.
- A work sample reads as: what the client needed, what was delivered, the result.
- Raise prices after the first few strong reviews.
