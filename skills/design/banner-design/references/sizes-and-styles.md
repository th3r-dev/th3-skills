# Sizes and styles

Exact canvas sizes per placement, and the art directions to choose from. Platforms change their
specs; when a size here matters for a launch, check the platform's own help page first.

## Code hosting

| Placement | Size (px) | Ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GitHub social preview (repo card) | 1280 × 640 | 2:1 | Min 640 × 320, under 1 MB. Some link unfurlers show it at 1.91:1 (≈ 1222 × 640, centred): keep core content 30px+ from the left and right |
| README hero | 1280 × 640-720 | 2:1-16:9 | Rendered at the column width (≈ 830px on desktop, ≈ 360px on a phone) |

## Brand-side layout per ratio

How the brand panel and the four steps of `../SKILL.md` fit each shape.

| Ratio | Brand panel | Four steps |
|---|---|---|
| 2:1 to 3:1 (GitHub, X, hero) | left 28-34% | 4 columns |
| 4:1 to 6:1 (LinkedIn, leaderboard) | left 20-25%, name + one line only | 4 columns, verb + one line, no detail |
| 16:9 (YouTube, event) | left, inside the safe area | 4 columns inside the safe area |
| 1:1, 4:5 (post) | top band | 2 × 2 grid |
| 9:16, 2:3 (story, pin) | top band | 4 stacked rows |
| 300 × 250 and smaller | brand + one step only | split into a 4-frame rotation instead |

## Art directions

Pick 2-3 that suit the brand and the audience, build each as a variant, and let the brief choose.
A brand that already has a look keeps it: these are for the gaps.

| # | Style | What makes it | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Minimalist | Lots of white space, one focal element, 1-2 colors, clean sans | SaaS, tech |
| 2 | Bold typography | Oversized type is the visual | Announcements |
| 3 | Gradient / color wash | Mesh gradients, blended hues | Modern brands |
| 4 | Photo-based | Full-bleed photo, text overlay | Lifestyle, e-commerce |
| 5 | Illustrated | Custom illustration and icons | Friendly products |
| 6 | Geometric / abstract | Shapes, lines, grids | Tech, fintech |
| 7 | Retro / vintage | Distressed texture, muted palette, serif, halftone | F&B, craft |
| 8 | Glassmorphism | Frosted panels, blur, glow borders | SaaS, apps |
| 9 | 3D / sculptural | Rendered objects, depth, shadow | Product, tech |
| 10 | Neon / cyberpunk | Dark ground, glowing accents | Gaming, events |
| 11 | Duotone | Photo in two brand colors | Bold brands |
| 12 | Editorial / magazine | Grid, pull quotes | Media, luxury |
| 13 | Collage / mixed media | Cut-paper, cutouts, layers | Culture, youth |
| 14 | Retro futurism | Chrome, gradients, space-age optimism | Launches |
| 15 | Anti-design | Deliberately broken grids, mixed fonts | Art, fashion |
| 16 | Digi-cute / kawaii | Rounded shapes, pastels, pixel art | Consumer apps |
| 17 | Tactile | Puffy, embossed, hyper-real materials | Consumer goods |
| 18 | Data / infographic | Numbers and charts are the hero | Reports, B2B |
| 19 | Dark / moody | Near-black, jewel tones, high contrast | Premium tech |
| 20 | Flat / solid color | One ground color, clean icons, no gradient | Dev tools, docs |
| 21 | Nature / organic | Earth tones, botanical motifs | Sustainability |
| 22 | Kinetic | Layered parts built to loop | Animated heroes |

## Research queries

When reference research is available (Inspo, Awwwards, Pinterest), collect 3-5 compositions:

- `[purpose] banner design [style]`: "github banner minimalist"
- `[platform] cover design inspiration`: "x header developer tool"
- `creative banner layout [industry]`: "banner layout developer tools"
- `[style] graphic design [year]`: "flat graphic design 2026"
